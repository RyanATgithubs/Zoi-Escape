Story

In a quiet rural town, a dark secret haunts the people who live there. Every month, groups of scientists accompanied by military personnel arrive without warning, demanding that each family surrender one child for a mysterious "research program." Refusal is not an option. Families who resist simply disappear. To protect his loved ones, a teenage boy named Zoi makes the painful decision to volunteer himself. He is taken far from home to a hidden underground laboratory where countless children are imprisoned and subjected to cruel experiments designed to create powerful biological weapons.

Deep beneath the surface, the laboratory becomes a place of fear and suffering. Scientists experiment on humans and animals alike, twisting them into monstrous creatures. Some experiments turn children into unstable test subjects, while animals mutate into violent zombie-like beasts. Zoi survives the harsh conditions longer than most, quietly observing the facility and waiting for an opportunity to escape. One day, a catastrophic experiment goes terribly wrong. A test subject breaks free from containment, spreading chaos through the laboratory and turning scientists and creatures alike into uncontrollable monsters. Within hours, the entire facility collapses into panic and destruction.

Amid the chaos, Zoi becomes one of the few survivors left alive. Knowing the laboratory will soon become a tomb, he begins a desperate escape through its dangerous levels—from the deepest research chambers to the surface above. Along the way he must fight mutated creatures, avoid deadly traps, and overcome powerful enemies guarding the path to freedom. Each floor of the laboratory reveals more horrifying truths about the experiments conducted there. With courage, determination, and the will to survive, Zoi pushes forward toward the final exit—hoping that if he can escape, he may finally expose the horrors hidden beneath the laboratory and reclaim the life that was stolen from him.
